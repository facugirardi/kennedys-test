# Award-winning luxury real estate and architecture web design (Awwwards and others, 2025–2026), as reference for the Kennedy's real estate section

Research date: 2026-10-20. Most data comes from Awwwards site pages, which list award, date, scores, tech tags, a 2-colour palette and "highlighted elements". Awwwards pages almost never list font names. CSSDA, FWA, Godly, SiteInspire and Lapa Ninja could not be checked directly (see Gaps).

## 1. Which real estate, residential and architecture sites won Awwwards SOTD or similar recently, and who designed them?

### Takeaway
Award-winning real estate sites in 2025–2026 come mostly from a small group of specialist studios: Vide Infra (at least 4 SOTDs for luxury residential sites), The First The Last (two Costa del Sol villa projects with SOTD and Developer awards), Square43, Unseen Studio and DEMARCA. Two winners are Mediterranean Spanish villa projects (ERA Residence and Horizonte Village), and they are the closest analogues to Kennedy's.

## 2. Per-site design breakdown (hero, typography, palette, layout, motion, browsing, CTA)

### Takeaway
The winners share a cinematic, chaptered scroll narrative. They open with a pre-loader or intro animation and a full-bleed video or render, then reveal architecture through scroll-scrubbed motion. They use restrained 2-tone palettes drawn from materials and landscape, and they treat the unit or property browser as a designed product (filters, 3D or visual selectors, masterplans), not a generic grid.

### Cited Findings
- **ERA Residence (Marbella):** highlighted elements are a Day/Night theme, horizontal scroll, pre-loader, gallery, property card, listing page, contact page and key features. The palette is pastel sky blue and pink, unusual for luxury and evoking Mediterranean dusk. Tags are Parallax, Storytelling and Photo & Video — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/era-residence)
- **Horizonte Village (Mijas):** highlighted elements are an Objects (villas) page, slider, video, contact form, gallery, map and "Quote Animation". Tags include 360 and Big Background Images. The palette is deep navy plus sand — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/horizonte-village)
- **Belgrade Arbor:** "cinematic storytelling, motion, and 3D design". Highlighted elements are a 3D experience, intro animation, residences page with **filters**, menu, smooth scroll, contact and 404 — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/belgrade-arbor)
- **Sobha Privy Collection:** product selector, 3D WebGL gallery, 3D WebGL map, "zoom on scroll", strict black/white — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/sobha-privy-collection)
- **Silver Pinewood:** a pastel palette, layered compositions and an even scroll rhythm. A "Territory" section uses timed animation and stone textures. The lobby's marble Zeus statue is rebuilt in WebGL and runs smoothly on phones — [Vide Infra](https://videinfra.com/blog/best-real-estate-websites-examples). Tagline: "True luxury is quiet" — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/silver-pinewood-residences)
- **Era (Vide Infra):** the hero is a roughly 8,000 px render with the camera scrolling across facade details. A WebGL "line of light" runs through the site as a visual thread. There is a live 3D neighbourhood map with moving cars, and the palette is drawn from Art Deco star patterns — [Vide Infra](https://videinfra.com/blog/best-real-estate-websites-examples)
- **LIKOVA:** a WebGL dusk render as the hero, a rotatable 3D building and site plan, and two unit selectors (one visual, one parametric with filters) — [Vide Infra](https://videinfra.com/blog/best-real-estate-websites-examples)
- **Springs:** a slowly shifting "hypnotic" hero, WebGL planes that dissolve into one another, a cursor-reactive interface, and copy that is soft in tone but sharpens into precise statements where facts are needed — [Vide Infra](https://videinfra.com/blog/best-real-estate-websites-examples)
- **Poklonnaya 9:** seasonal visual states (darker autumn and winter, lighter spring and summer), a "Spend a day" section that runs from morning to night, and visual apartment selection with parameter filters — [Vide Infra](https://videinfra.com/blog/best-real-estate-websites-examples)
- **AIR (Vide Infra):** frosted semi-transparent panels that echo the glass facade, and a configurator where adjacent offices merge with live area and price — [Vide Infra](https://videinfra.com/blog/best-real-estate-websites-examples)
- **Quinta D. Amália:** a pre-launch site with no building to photograph ("The website had to become the property before the first stone"). Single page; hero, apartments list, footer; near-monochrome #262626/#F4F4F4 — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/quinta-d-amalia)
- **Loam House:** "An immersive, chaptered scroll journey… revealed through motion". Tags are Parallax, Typography, Transitions and Gestures — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/loam-house)
- **360 Lexington:** hero, building highlights, location and lifestyle, parallax gallery, and an available offices and plans section — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/360-lexington-ave)
- **21 Oaks:** a "bold editorial approach"; scroll-driven homepage, apartments, gallery, maps — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/21-oaks)
- **Lenval Properties:** fullscreen layouts, typography, microinteractions, a dedicated masterplan page, amenities, a preloader and a project page — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/lenval-properties)
- **Mejuma Tuscany:** big background images, a loader, navigation and mouse-hover details. "Timeless Italian character". This is the closest analogue to a European heritage or holiday-home sales brand — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/mejuma-tuscany)
- **25 Residences:** tagged Typography and Graphic design, with a deep green and warm grey palette — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/25-residences)

### Inferences
- Recurring palette logic is one dark "ink" plus one warm light neutral: navy/sand, forest/cream, umber/bone, charcoal/taupe. Mediterranean projects lean toward sea-navy, sand and dusk pastels. For Kennedy's (Aegean islands, Riviera): whitewash and limestone off-white, a deep Aegean navy, and possibly one sun-faded accent (terracotta or dusk pink, as ERA Residence does).
- Common page architecture is pre-loader, full-bleed hero, a key-features or "lifestyle" chapter, residences or objects listing with filters, a single property page (gallery, plans, map), a location map, and a contact or enquiry page. The pieces are standard; what wins is the motion and the art direction.
- No inspected site advertises a chat-first or aggressive CTA. Enquiry appears to live on a dedicated contact page or form and on property cards. This is inferred from the highlighted-element lists and is not verified on the live sites.

### Gaps
- Exact hero media (video vs. render vs. WebGL) and CTA placement for ERA Residence and Horizonte Village were not verified on the live sites. Visit them to confirm.
- Font names were not available.

## 3. Common trends, tasteful vs. gimmicky, and performance and accessibility trade-offs

### Takeaway
The dominant 2025–2026 trend is "quiet luxury plus cinematic scroll": restrained 2-colour palettes, editorial typography, big imagery, and storytelling through motion. Heavy WebGL and 3D is common among Vide Infra and Dubai winners but is costly. Awwwards accessibility sub-scores for these sites are consistently the weakest.

### Cited Findings
- Trend tags that recur across winners are Storytelling, Parallax, Scrolling, Big Background Images, Typography, Transitions, 3D/WebGL and pre-loaders — [ERA Residence](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/era-residence), [Loam House](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/loam-house), [Horizonte](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/horizonte-village), [Lenval](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/lenval-properties)
- Accessibility sub-scores are often the lowest Dev metric: Horizonte 6.60, Belgrade Arbor 6.80, Sobha 7.20, ERA Residence 7.20. The 25 Residences site (7.40) is an exception — see the respective Awwwards pages ([Horizonte](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/horizonte-village), [Belgrade Arbor](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/belgrade-arbor)).
- WPO (performance) sub-scores range from 7.0 to 7.8. The best-performing site was Silver Pinewood (7.80) despite its WebGL — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/silver-pinewood-residences)
- Vide Infra's brand line on Silver Pinewood is "True luxury is quiet" — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/silver-pinewood-residences)
- Day/night theme toggles (ERA Residence) and seasonal theming (Poklonnaya 9) are emerging "living site" ideas — [Awwwards ERA Residence](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/era-residence), [Vide Infra](https://videinfra.com/blog/best-real-estate-websites-examples)

### Inferences (editorial judgement, not sourced)
- **Tasteful:**
  - slow and short image reveals (clip-path or mask) and subtle parallax on photography
  - smooth scroll with native scrollbar semantics
  - oversized serif or display headings paired with a neutral grotesk
  - chaptered scroll stories on single property pages
  - restrained palettes
  - horizontal galleries used sparingly
  - a calm pre-loader only if it is under about 1 s
- **Gimmicky or risky:**
  - long or blocking pre-loaders
  - custom cursors that replace the native one
  - scroll-jacking that changes scroll speed or direction
  - heavy WebGL on listing pages
  - 3D building models for resale villas, where no CAD model exists anyway. Kennedy's sells individual villas, not off-plan towers, so 3D unit selectors and masterplans are mostly irrelevant. Photography and video should carry the experience.
- **Performance:** a full-bleed hero video should be a short muted loop, delivered as a poster image plus lazy-loaded video, with `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured. Keep WebGL off the listing and search pages that SEO and conversion depend on.

### Gaps
- No quantitative conversion or lead data was found linking any of these award sites to business results.

## 4. Which techniques are realistic in Next.js 14 + Tailwind, and which libraries do winners use?

### Takeaway
GSAP (with ScrollTrigger) is the most common identifiable motion library among the winners. Webflow is the most common platform, and one SOTD (Belgrade Arbor) is built on Next.js + GSAP + Three.js, which proves the target stack works at award level. Lenis is the de-facto smooth-scroll layer.

### Cited Findings
- **Belgrade Arbor:** Next.js, GSAP, Three.js (SOTD + Dev, Oct 2026) — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/belgrade-arbor)
- **ERA Residence:** GSAP + Webflow — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/era-residence)
- **Lenval Properties:** Webflow, GSAP, WebGL — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/lenval-properties)
- **Mejuma Tuscany:** Webflow, GSAP — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/mejuma-tuscany)
- **21 Oaks:** Webflow, GSAP — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/21-oaks)
- **Loam House:** HTML5, JavaScript, GSAP — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/loam-house)
- **Sobha Privy Collection:** Three.js/WebGL — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/sobha-privy-collection)
- **360 Lexington:** Framer — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/360-lexington-ave)
- **Lenis:**
  - open-source smooth scroll by darkroom.engineering, under 5 kb, set up with one `new Lenis()` call
  - offers snap (`lenis/snap`), horizontal and infinite scroll
  - adds no CSS transforms and doesn't hijack the scrollbar; the vendor claims "no accessibility trade-offs"
  - used by Unseen Studio (the studio behind the 25 Residences SOTD), the GTA VI site, Netflix careers and others
  - it powers Locomotive Scroll
  - [lenis.dev](https://lenis.dev/)

### Inferences
- **Realistic for Kennedy's (Next.js 14 Pages Router + Tailwind):**
  - Lenis for smooth scroll
  - GSAP + ScrollTrigger for pinned, chaptered sections, scroll-scrubbed image sequences and horizontal galleries (GSAP is now free, including its plugins, after the Webflow acquisition; this is from background knowledge and should be verified)
  - Framer Motion for page transitions, menus and micro-interactions on React components
  - CSS clip-path or mask image reveals
  - `next/image` for responsive photography
  - three.js or react-three-fiber only for an optional, isolated hero moment
- **Recommended priority:** Lenis + GSAP for the editorial property story pages, and Framer Motion for UI state. Avoid running two scroll-driven systems at once; make GSAP ScrollTrigger the single scroll source, synced with Lenis.
- **Pages Router caveat:** page transitions need an `AnimatePresence` wrapper in `_app.tsx`. GSAP code must run in `useEffect` or `useLayoutEffect` with cleanup (`gsap.context().revert()`) to avoid SSR and hydration issues.

### Gaps
- No library data was found for Horizonte Village, Silver Pinewood, Era or 25 Residences, whose Awwwards pages list only generic tags.
- GSAP licensing status was not re-verified in this session.

## 5. Mediterranean and coastal-themed award winners specifically

### Takeaway
The strongest Mediterranean references are both on Spain's Costa del Sol, by the same studio (The First The Last): ERA Residence (Marbella/Estepona, SOTD and Developer award, Aug 2026) and Horizonte Village (Mijas, SOTD Mar 2025). No recent Greek-island (Mykonos, Paros) or French Riviera real estate site with an Awwwards SOTD was found. Mejuma Tuscany (2026 nominee) and Quinta D. Amália (Portugal, Honorable Mention 2026) are the nearest southern-European references.

### Cited Findings
- **ERA Residence:** "Mediterranean-style boutique residence project on the New Golden Mile, near Marbella and Estepona, blending architecture, natural materials, and resort-style living". Built with GSAP and Webflow. Has a Day/Night theme and horizontal scroll — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/era-residence)
- **Horizonte Village:** "luxury residential project in Mijas, Costa del Sol, with modern villas, sea views, smart home technology, and private pools". Palette #21343E / #DFD8CF; 360 views, map and video — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/horizonte-village)
- **Mejuma Tuscany:** Italian homes, "timeless Italian character", Webflow and GSAP, Nominee Oct 2026 — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/mejuma-tuscany)
- **Quinta D. Amália:** Portugal, Honorable Mention Sep 2026 — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/quinta-d-amalia)
- **Greece:** only older entries were found (Olympus Villas, Honorable Mention 2017) — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/olympus-villas). A search for Mykonos, Paros or Athens award winners returned no Awwwards real estate results.

### Inferences
- There is a whitespace opportunity: no Greek-island luxury property sales site appears among recent award winners. A Kennedy's redesign with Aegean art direction (whitewash, stone, deep blue, light and shadow motion) would be distinctive in the category.
- ERA Residence and Horizonte Village should be the first two sites the design team studies live, focusing on how they present individual villas (the "Objects" page, property card, listing page) rather than towers.

### Gaps
- FWA, CSS Design Awards, Godly, SiteInspire and Lapa Ninja could not be queried effectively. The SiteInspire fetch returned no content and the Godly search returned irrelevant results. Their Mediterranean and real estate collections remain unchecked.
- No French Riviera or Ibiza real estate award winners were found.
